# Landscape composition plan

Presentation only. Scenario geometry continues to define route weights. All pre-change rendered edge lengths will be retained as delivery timing lengths.

Riverside: retain its west-bank settlement and east-bank branch, with the continuous north–south river and E–F crossing unchanged.

## Twin Districts

A north-to-south river divides around a long, uneven island. The narrower west arm crosses C–D; the stronger east channel crosses D–E. D occupies a raised central clearing. Western roads skirt a wooded hollow; the eastern settlement follows an oblique ridge.

## Lifeline

The main dry valley descends south through the map. Two tributary gullies erode the north and south sides of E’s plateau; their narrow heads climb east and stop in rocky slopes before the F–G ridge road. D–E spans the main valley, E–F the north gully, and E–G the south gully.

## Crossfire

B stands on a western upland spur above a connected ravine with a tapering inland headwall. D and E occupy the central ridge. East of them a substantial lake fills an irregular basin and drains north through both D–F and E–F crossings. The ordinary A–C–E–G–H–F route goes around the valley head and southern lakeshore on continuous land.
