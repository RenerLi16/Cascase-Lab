# Cascade Lab — companion city maps

Three standalone illustrations created with the built-in imagegen tool, using the Scenario 2–4 landmark names and connections in the local scenario files. They match the overhead, light architectural style of the Riverside illustration. No changes were made to the running game or scenario data.

The roads and landmark campuses were visually reviewed against their scenario graphs. Artwork positions are approximate; integrating these images into the game will require landmark and road registration, as the existing Riverside artwork does.

Preserve detail and lightness together. Multiple roads ending at a node meet in that same local forecourt/junction. Every edge is one continuous two-way road from node to node. Bend edges gently within their corridors for a natural urban feel, but do not move landmarks to different regions. Do not omit roads, add extra arterial connections, share a trunk away from a node, or introduce non-node crossings. All local neighborhood accesses must be short dead-end access lanes or pedestrian alleys, with no competing continuous through-road that bypasses the required network. Complete neighborhoods should fill the space between the nine/eight landmarks, not isolated buildings floating in green fields. Eight landmark campuses total. Coordinates below use a 1000 x 700 logical map, with x from left and y from top; scale proportionally to the output. Names and node IDs are instructions ONLY, never text in the artwork.
